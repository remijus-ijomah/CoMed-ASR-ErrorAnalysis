CoMed-ASR-ErrorAnalysis



CoMed-ASR is a research project investigating automatic speech recognition (ASR) errors in Nigerian medical conversations, with particular attention to Nigerian-accented English and English–Nigerian Pidgin code-switching.



Project Status



🚧 Work in progress.



Research Objective



RO1: To systematically analyze and characterize ASR errors in Nigerian-accented English medical conversations and English–Nigerian Pidgin code-switched conversations.



Research Focus



The project focuses on:



Systematically analyzing ASR transcription errors.



Identifying and characterizing substitution, deletion, and insertion errors.



Investigating ASR errors involving medical terminology.



Examining the impact of Nigerian-accented English on ASR performance.



Analyzing errors in English–Nigerian Pidgin code-switched speech.



Evaluating ASR performance across relevant datasets, speakers, and conversational conditions.



Developing reproducible tools and methodologies for ASR error analysis.



Repository Structure



The repository structure and documentation will be developed as the research progresses.
